This dataset folder keeps the minimal branch used for the CONA
ligand-decoupling-from-complex benchmark:

- system/: extracted from coordinates-and-topologies/1/r1/ligand-decoupling-from-complex
- mdp/: extracted from MDPs/ligand-decoupling-from-complex/coulomb
- raw/: original downloaded archives kept for provenance

Additional local files were added to make the benchmark reproducible:

- system/charmm36-feb2021.ff/: force-field include tree referenced by the topology
- reproduce/: numbered launcher scripts for TPR generation, 100-conf generation,
  force evaluation, and MPI timing
